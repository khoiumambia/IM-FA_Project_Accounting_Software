about AcctBook

Your trusted partner for professional accounting software solutions since 2020

Our Mission

To simplify accounting for businesses of all sizes with intuitive, powerful, and accessible software solutions.

Our Vision

To become the leading accounting platform in Bangladesh, empowering thousands of businesses with financial clarity.

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, and customer success drive everything we do at AcctBook.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, AcctBook began with a simple idea: make double-entry accounting accessible to everyone. What started as a small project has grown into a comprehensive accounting solution used by businesses across Bangladesh. Our software combines traditional accounting principles with modern technology to deliver accurate, real-time financial insights.

Why Choose Us

✓ Double-entry accounting system
✓ Comprehensive reporting (Ledger, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet)
✓ Bank and cash management
✓ Group classification for detailed analysis
✓ User-friendly interface with keyboard navigation
✓ Free and open for all businesses
